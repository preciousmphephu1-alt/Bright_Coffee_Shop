# Bright_Coffee_Shop
Bright Coffee Shop Sales Analysis 

Project Overview 

The Bright Coffee Shop Sales Analysis project focuses on analysing historical sales data to understand business performance, identify sales trends, and provide actionable recommendations to improve revenue and product performance. 

The analysis covers the period 1 January 2023 to 30 June 2023 and examines revenue, quantity sold, transaction activity, product categories, store locations, and customer purchasing patterns. 

The purpose of this project is to transform raw sales data into meaningful insights that support data-driven decision-making by management. 

Business Objectives 

Analyse overall sales and revenue performance. 

Identify the highest-performing and lowest-performing months. 

Determine which product categories and products generate the most revenue. 

Compare sales performance across store locations. 

Analyse sales by day of the week and time of day. 

Examine the relationship between quantity sold and revenue. 

Provide recommendations to support inventory planning, staffing, and promotional decisions. 

Tools and Technologies 

Excel: Data review, PivotTables, PivotCharts, and dashboard development. 

SQL / Databricks: Querying, analysing, and aggregating sales data. 

Power BI: Interactive dashboard and visual reporting. 

Looker Studio(Data studio): Interactive dashboard with filters, KPIs, and sales charts. 

Lovable: Development of a sales dashboard and presentation of business insights. 

Miro: Planning the project workflow and data architecture. 

Microsoft PowerPoint: Communicating findings and recommendations to management.

Methodology 

Data preparation: Reviewed the dataset and prepared the fields required for analysis. 

Data analysis: Used SQL queries and Excel calculations to aggregate revenue, transaction counts, and quantities sold. 

Exploratory analysis: Compared sales across months, products, stores, weekdays, weekends, and time periods. 

Dashboard development: Created charts, KPI cards, and interactive filters to explore business performance. 

Business insights: Interpreted the results and developed recommendations based on the observed sales patterns. 

Presentation: Prepared a management-focused PowerPoint presentation to communicate the findings to the ceo. 

Key Performance Indicators 

Total Revenue: 698,812.33 

Total Quantity Sold: 214,470 

Total Transactions: 149,116 

Average Revenue per Transaction: approximately 4.69 

These figures reflect the January–June 2023 reporting period and the calculations used in the dashboard. 

Key Findings 

Monthly performance: June recorded the highest revenue, while February recorded the lowest. 

Product category: Coffee was the leading category by revenue. 

Store performance: Hell's Kitchen generated the highest revenue among the three store locations. 

Time of day: Morning was the strongest sales period. 

Product performance: Barista Espresso led in revenue, while Brewed Chai Tea and Gourmet brewed coffee were among the leading products by quantity sold. 

Recommendations 

Monitor stock levels for popular products to reduce the risk of stock shortages. 

Align staffing and product preparation with peak morning demand. 

Investigate the practices contributing to stronger performance at Hell's Kitchen. 

Test-targeted promotions during slower sales periods. 

Monitor revenue, quantity sold, and average transaction value regularly to measure the impact of business decisions.

Conclusion 

This project demonstrates how data analysis and visualisation can be used to evaluate coffee shop sales performance. By identifying revenue trends, high-performing products, store differences, and peak sales periods, the analysis provides management with information to support better operational and promotional decisions. 

The recommendations should be monitored over time to determine whether they improve business performance.
